Pickpockets

Pickpockets work the busiest tourist spots, the Métro, RER trains and big stations. Stops in these areas get a reminder, and the Pickpockets chip on the map shows them.

  • Keep bags zipped and in front; phones and wallets out of back pockets.
  • Keep passports and cards in separate places.
  • Be wary of crowding at lift and escalator queues, and of petitions or "found ring" approaches.

How alerts work

Red An operator notice cites a demonstration within 500 m, now or in the next 2 hours.

Amber Within 2 km, within 1 km later today, or a Métro/RER line blocked within 500 m.

Clear Nothing like that in the feeds. Feeds can miss things, so stay alert.

News Press reports are unconfirmed. One within 1 km in the last 6 hours makes a place amber, never red.

Strikes are shown separately and never turn a place red. For a stop, the check covers one hour before to two hours after its time.
