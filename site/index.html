Get a phone notification when one of your places turns amber or red. Works while the app is open or recently used.

How the colours work

Red
An operator notice cites a demonstration or strike within 500 m of a place, now or in the next 2 hours.
Amber
The same within 2 km, a demonstration within 1 km later today, or a Métro/RER line blocked within 500 m.
Green
Nothing like that in the feeds right now. Feeds can miss things, so stay alert.
